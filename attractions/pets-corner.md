# Pets Corner

## Description
Small fenced partition with battery-operated or regular pet dolls (dogs, cats), soft pet houses, a brush for kids to use, and toy pet food. Houses ~8–10 pets in total.

## Space Requirements
- Footprint: 2m x 2m
- Clearance: Low height (fence only)

## Popularity
Very popular with little girls

## Cost Estimate
- Small battery-operated pets: ~¥1,700 each (8–10 units ≈ ¥13,600–17,000)
- Large battery-operated pets: ~¥3,500 each (2 units ≈ ¥7,000)
- Soft pet houses: ~¥1,500 each (3 units ≈ ¥4,500)
- **Subtotal:** ~¥25,000–29,000 (excluding fencing materials)
